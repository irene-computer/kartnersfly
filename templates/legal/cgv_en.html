Prices are shown in CFA francs (FCFA), all taxes included, with an indicative equivalent in euros (€1 = 655.957 FCFA). They may change without notice. The final price is the one confirmed at the time of booking.

Article 3 - Booking and payment

A deposit of 30% of the total amount is required for every booking. The balance must be paid no later than 15 days before departure. Payment can be made by bank transfer, card or cash.

Article 4 - Cancellation and refunds
